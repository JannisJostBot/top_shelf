MENU · THE SCREENS BEHIND H

Direction H is a printed matchday program: the game's title art as the cover, the rink as the menu, the ticket stub to play. Below: how its sections open and close (animated), and every page behind it, plus the two in-game pages (pause and the result), so the whole menu is one consistent thing. Mock-ups only.

The rules every page follows: a page is a page of the program (paper, the arena's two inks, its number and title printed big); "‹ RINK" in the masthead goes back; the ticket stub stays at the bottom on every menu page, so Play is always one tap away; and the in-game pages are inserts printed on the game's dark stock.

1 · HOW IT MOVES

Each phone loops its transition. Every move is short (a quarter to half a second) and stepped, in keeping with the pixel art.
