TOP SHELF · HUD CONCEPTS

Three directions for a more characterful HUD. Each shows the phone top bar over the ice (with buttons, boost and player card), the desktop side panel and a couple of in-game pieces. Mix and match is fine, e.g. the jumbotron scoreboard with broadcast-style side panels. Colours follow the selected jerseys and arena in the real game.

A · JUMBOTRON the arena's own scoreboard, hanging over the ice

Steel housing with bolts and two cables, black LED panels with a dot-matrix grid. Scores glow amber, the clock red, the period is three lamps. Everything reads as hardware in the arena rather than an app overlay.

A2 · JUMBOTRON, READABLE same idea, tuned for legibility

The dot-matrix grid is now a faint texture behind the glyphs instead of a mask over them, so the digits stay solid. Scores and clock are larger, the glow is tighter, team tags are bigger with a shadow, the period is spelled out next to its lamps, and small text (positions, stats, the feed) is plain warm white rather than dotted LED.

GOAL!goal banner on an LED board
  • Contrast: amber on near-black, no grid over the glyphs; the dimmest text is still warm white.
  • Size: scores 24 px (34 on desktop), clock 16 px, tags 9 px; the bar grows by about 4 px.
  • Kept: housing, bolts, cables, amber/red LED colours and the period lamps.

A3 · JUMBOTRON, TRUE DOT-MATRIX the LED grid kept, but lined up with the letters

Every pixel of the font becomes one round LED, so the dots draw the digits instead of slicing through them. Big numbers use sizes on the font's 8-pixel grid (24 px scores, 16 px clock, 40 px on desktop), unlit LEDs glimmer faintly across each panel, and small secondary text stays plain so it remains readable.

DESKTOP · SIDE PANEL

GOAL!goal banner, one LED per font pixel
  • Why it reads: the dot pitch equals one font pixel, so no letter is ever cut; the gaps fall between pixels.
  • Rule for the game: LED text only at 16, 24, 32 or 40 px; anything smaller stays solid.
  • Glow: a soft drop shadow around the lit dots, plus faint unlit dots across each panel.

B · BOARDS & GLASS the HUD is built from the rink itself

The top bar is a stretch of dasher board: glass on top, white board face with puck scuffs, team ad panels and the yellow kick plate in the arena's colour. The clock sits in the penalty box window. Buttons are pucks.
