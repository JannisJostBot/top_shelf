SOUND LAB

Every sound in Top Shelf is synthesised live. For each effect, ▶ plays a variant (press it a few times: each play varies a little, as in the game). Pick the one you like; option A is always what the game plays before the first round of picks. Your picks are listed at the bottom. Tell me which ones you want, and only those will be built into the game.

Built into the game: strides B Carve, shot C Composite crack, pass B Tock, body check C Heavy (in the real hits), and pitch and level variation. Everything else stayed A.

Crowd reactions that some effects trigger in the game (the "ooh" after a check, the cheer after the horn) are left out here, so you only hear the effect itself.
